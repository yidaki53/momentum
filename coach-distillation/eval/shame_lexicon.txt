# Shame lexicon -- the dignity axis fails an item on ANY hit.

# Sourced from momentum/llm/knowledge.py ("What NOT to Say to Someone with Executive
# Dysfunction") plus the phrasings that actually appeared in our own failure logs.
#
# This is not a politeness list. Shame is a clinical risk factor: it deepens the
# procrastinate -> shame -> procrastinate cycle the app exists to break. A reply that
# scores well on every other axis and contains one of these has failed.
#
# Lower-case, one per line. '#' comments and blank lines are ignored.
#
# Deliberately NOT included: bare nouns like "failure" or "lazy". A coach that says
# "that is not a moral failure" or "laziness is a load-shedding behaviour" is doing
# the validating job correctly. Phrasings that assert the deficiency are listed; the
# word alone is not, because a substring match cannot tell the two apart.
