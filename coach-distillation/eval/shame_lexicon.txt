# --- patronising register -------------------------------------------------
# v2 scored dignity 1.00 while replying to g02: "Take a deep breath, dear
# friend. Remember, every setback is an opportunity to learn and grow stronger.
# Keep pushing forward!"
#
# Not one of the phrases above fires, and it passed. That is the failure mode this
# list had: it only knows the phrasings that were anticipated. Patronising warmth is
# harder to catch than hostility because it contains no accusation and no imperative --
# it is the tone of someone being kind at you rather than with you. "Every setback is
# an opportunity" in particular tells a person with executive dysfunction that their
# difficulty is a lesson, which is exactly the reframe the app exists to dismantle.
#
